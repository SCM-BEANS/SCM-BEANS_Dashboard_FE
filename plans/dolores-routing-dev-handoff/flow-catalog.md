# Dolores flow catalog: 20 main flows / 45 scenarios

**Nguồn nghiệp vụ:** SRS EN v1.1, Section 4 and Section 10 traceability; MainFlow corrected; confirmed decisions U-01…U-11; SLA tracking scope newly requested 2026-10-14 and detailed in `service-sla-management.md`.  
**Cách đọc:** MF/S is the source Main Flow/Scenario ID. Routes below are the existing prototype entry points; where a route currently lacks a record detail, use a selected-record panel or define a record URL without changing business sequence. Every state-changing step must identify the source record and authenticated actor in the API.

## Luồng chính theo thứ tự

| Flow | Actors and route entry | Scenario sequence and expected result |
|---|---|---|
| **MF-01 Onboarding and rental contract** | Admin (shop/Tenant access); Tenant, Moderator; /admin/stores, /admin/accounts → /moderator/customers → /moderator/contracts/new → /moderator/contracts/:contractId; Tenant /customer/contracts | **S01:** Tenant states rental need; Moderator checks the Tenant's existing account/shop and eligible inventory, then agrees machine type, period, installation location and contractual amounts. Admin creates/manages shops, sets Tenant status and grants Tenant access; Moderator cannot perform those actions. Smart IoT prerequisites are assessed internally and must pass before the Moderator proceeds. **S02:** If needed, Moderator may create a Tenant account, but this does not create a shop or grant shop access. Admin performs the shop/status/access actions. Moderator records one contract and one primary physical machine, attaches the externally signed agreement and exposes the record only to the correct Tenant. No e-sign. Contract and machine IDs remain separate; no second active primary assignment. Tenant↔shop cardinality and exact account-to-shop setup sequence remain Cần chốt. |
| **MF-02 Conventional delivery and handover** | Moderator, Technician, Tenant; /moderator/technical-requests/new → /technician/tasks/:taskId → /moderator/machines/:machineId or /customer/machines/:machineId | **S01:** Moderator requests delivery against a saved contract and dispatches Technician with the exact machine, address and contract IDs. **S02:** Technician records delivery/installation/handover against that job; system appends machine history, and Tenant/Moderator can view contractual location and result. Do not display online state, cups, GPS, Global Menu, remote menu or Smart telemetry for this machine. |
| **MF-03 Smart IoT delivery and activation** | Moderator, Technician, Tenant, Machine; same technical-task entry; Admin/Moderator monitoring and Customer machine detail | **S01:** Moderator assigns a Smart IoT unit only after agreed network prerequisites; Technician records delivery and device/contract association. **S02:** Technician verifies the factory-provided Global Menu exists/applies and records activation/network outcome. Tenant sees correct Smart device and contract. Device-confirmed menu/telemetry is shown with last sync. Do not treat a cloud push as necessary to prove factory menu presence. |
| **MF-04 First-month bill and first payment** | System generates/drafts bill; Moderator /moderator/invoices; Tenant /customer/invoices | **S01:** Build itemized first period from contract, rental duration, agreed installation and applicable charges; show VND 10M deposit separately. Use VND 1.5M conventional or Smart VND 750K base + VND 1K valid cup. Do not claim a bill was reconciled revenue. **S02:** Tenant transfers outside system, uploads evidence and clicks Confirm; save one immutable submission event and timestamp. Moderator compares with internal bank data, saves company-side evidence and reconciliation result. Reconciliation and tenant confirmation are distinct events. |
| **MF-05 Recurring bill and payment exceptions** | System, Moderator, Tenant; /moderator/invoices ↔ /customer/invoices | **S01:** Issue ordinary bills on calendar month end. Attach usage to the correct machine/contract and list agreed fees. Late-arriving ordinary-period cups move to next month; never revise/reissue the previously issued bill for those cups. **S02:** Tenant uploads proof + confirms; reminders and daily compounding stop at that submission time. Moderator reconciles later. Within due term, request the confirmed short amount. For a confirmed overpayment, Moderator arranges external refund and records its independent transaction/evidence. Exception behavior when Moderator rejects proof or finds a short amount after submission remains OI-07. |
| **MF-06 Smart PAYG and delayed synchronization** | Machine/Cloud, System, Moderator, Tenant; /admin/monitoring → customer/machine; /customer/machines/:machineId/telemetry | **S01:** Machine reports successful sold-cup events with occurrence and source identity; Edge stores events when offline and syncs later. Cloud displays received count and last sync; do not estimate missing cups from offline days. Ordinary month late events carry forward. Final contract bill waits until all events through use cutoff are synchronized, including zero-sales days and offline periods; delayed sync must not extend rental. **S02:** Attribute cups from a same-type Smart replacement to the primary contract only for that replacement's actual assignment interval; prevent duplicate or cross-period billing. Device proof of completeness, event deduplication, and boundary handling are open integration matters. |
| **MF-07 Global Menu and private recipes** | Admin/Moderator /admin/global-menu or /moderator/global-menu; Tenant /customer/menu and /customer/machines/:machineId | **S01:** Internal staff revise/version the Dolores Global Menu; only Admin/Moderator can author it. **S02:** Production delivery/rollout to Smart machines is gated by the approved device capability, versioning, offline queue and retry contract (OI-03/OI-12). Until that contract is approved and the device confirms application, keep showing the last device-confirmed menu; a UI “sent” state must not imply application. **S03:** Tenant edits a private recipe derived from Global; validate required ingredients/quantities against device capability. **S04:** Tenant adds a private drink only if selected machine declares it supported. Global changes preserve private values; Tenant can see the new base and explicitly choose to adopt it. No automatic overwrite and no invented Moderator approval. Conventional machines have no Dolores menu. |
| **MF-08 Preventive maintenance record** | Moderator, Technician; /moderator/technical-requests → /technician/tasks/:taskId → machine history | **S01:** Company remains responsible for planning the every-three-month/ten-day-window maintenance; Dolores records the applicable approved maintenance window/SLA only when its anchor and target are defined. It does not create an employee calendar, appointment, automatic assignment or maintenance reminder. **S02:** Moderator manually coordinates/records the actual job and Technician; Technician records actual work/result, parts/costs per contract and machine/temporary replacement handovers. Compare actual milestone/completion timestamps with an approved target when available; otherwise label SLA as not configured. Append linked histories. If replacement is used, type must match and later return closes only that assignment. |
| **MF-09 Breakdown and repair** | Tenant, Moderator, Technician; /customer/support → /moderator/technical-requests/new → task detail and /technician/tasks/:taskId | **S01:** Tenant selects the actual in-use machine, describes fault and optionally attaches evidence; including active replacement when applicable. System timestamps the request and snapshots any applicable approved SLA terms; the source of target (contract/service policy) is Cần chốt. Missing terms are shown as “Chưa cấu hình SLA,” with no invented default. Moderator receives that exact support request, sees confirmed response/service deadlines and manually coordinates a Technician. **S02:** Assigned Technician sees the task and approved SLA target, records actual acknowledgement/start/diagnosis milestones that the policy requires; criteria stay internal and charges follow contract. No calendar booking or automatic technician selection. **S03:** Technician records repair/parts/completion and return/replacement interval; update the original support request and append machine history. Compare the required actual milestone with its confirmed deadline and expose due/breached result. Tenant can follow status and the customer-visible commitment as later confirmed. Do not convert every parts change into an automatic bill. Exact SLA targets, clock/pause rules and alerts remain Cần chốt (OI-19). |
| **MF-10 Contract expiry reminder/decision** | System, Moderator, Tenant; /moderator/contracts/:contractId and /customer/contracts/:contractId | **S01:** At one month before end, remind Tenant and Moderator to ask about continuing; delivery channel/timing remain open. **S02:** Record Tenant's response. No response means no continued rental when the existing contract ends. A positive interest creates a discussion/new-contract path only; do not silently renew. |
| **MF-11 Normal expiry, recovery, deposit** | Moderator, Technician, Tenant; contract detail → recovery task → /moderator/deposits → bill/refund view | **S01:** Moderator asks Tenant to prepare the contracted machine. Technician collects and assesses the physical machine and records result. **S02:** After that result, Moderator settles per deposit actually received, outstanding bills and contract provisions, handles damage/violation per agreement, informs Tenant and tracks any external refund. Damage is deducted from deposit; excess is recorded owed. Eligible remainder refund deadline is ten working days from Moderator notification. Must not use an assessment from another contract. Conflict between still-pending final bill and refund deadline remains open. |
| **MF-12 Early termination** | Tenant, Moderator, Technician; /customer/contracts/:id → /moderator/contracts/:id → recovery task and settlement | **S01:** Record request, effective rental/use stop and agreed decision; do not derive stop time from click time if agreement says otherwise. Technician still recovers/assesses before deposit settlement. **S02:** For a multi-month rental, ending-month base rent follows full ending-month rule; valid cups stop at actual use cutoff. For a contract started and ended in the same month, rent uses actual rental time. Formula, inclusive dates and rounding remain OI-05. Apply deductions only by agreement and OI-14. |
| **MF-13 Contract violation** | Moderator, Technician, Tenant; /moderator/contracts/:id and recovery task | **S01:** Moderator records violation and applicable contract reference, then tracks/request recovery; Dolores does not enforce the contract or automatically lock the machine. **S02:** Technician recovers and assesses. Moderator applies only agreed liability/deductions and records eligible remainder or additional amount owed. User non-cooperation is handled by contract/company process, not an invented application workflow. |
| **MF-14 Continued rental through a new contract** | Tenant, Moderator, Technician; old contract detail → /moderator/contracts/new | **S01:** If Tenant wants service after expiry, negotiate and sign a separate external agreement. **S02:** Complete old contract's recovery/settlement lifecycle; independently allocate an eligible machine (it may differ), record new dates/terms and upload its signed agreement. | **MF-16 Replacement during maintenance/repair** | Moderator, Technician; /moderator/machines → work request and task | **S01:** Choose an actually available same-type machine; create a temporary interval linked to primary contract/work, not new contract. ## Actor responsibility rule

| Actor | Creates/changes | Reviews/sees | Explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | All three account roles; create/manage shops; set Tenant status and grant Tenant access; Global Menu; confirmed system settings/data. | Aggregate operations/revenue; customers, shops, contracts, machine/inventory/history and Smart telemetry. | Exact account status values/transitions and Tenant↔shop cardinality/setup sequence remain Cần chốt. |
| Moderator | Create Tenant accounts; view/manage customer contracts within scope; bill records; company reconciliation records; dispatch requests; Global Menu; contract/buyback tracking. | Customer operational records and relevant evidence, bank-side proof/internal reconciliation. | Cannot create Moderator/Technician accounts, administer shops, change Tenant status or grant access; does not perform actual bank transfers or contract enforcement. |
| Technician | Result for assigned work: delivery/handover, assessment, repair, maintenance, collection, refurbishment/testing. | Assigned tasks and relevant machine history. | Company scoring/checklist is out of platform scope. Test result must not itself release stock. |
| Tenant / Customer | Own payment submission/confirmation, support request, private recipes, buyback evidence acknowledgement, expiry response. | Own contracts, machine/telemetry, bills, assigned Global base/applied menu, request/result and own buyback evidence. | Cannot edit Global Menu/company bank evidence or another Tenant record. |
| System | Issue bills/reminders under confirmed conditions; aggregate usage; record device reports and history; enforce transition guards. | Derived views/notifications based on stored state. | No assumed bank APIs, invented sensors, automatic contract penalties or maintenance scheduling. |
| Smart Coffee Machine / Edge | Reports successful sales, connection/device/GPS/menu application result; caches offline data and syncs later. | Receives Global updates/private supported recipe changes. | A command sent is not an applied menu; last confirmed device state remains shown until device confirms. |

## Business narratives

### One rental lifecycle

Tenant expresses rental need; Moderator checks the Tenant account/shop and eligible inventory, agrees terms, and records the request. Admin creates/manages the shop, sets Tenant status and grants Tenant access; Moderator may create the Tenant account but cannot perform those Admin actions. If a new shop or access grant is needed, those actions belong to Admin; the exact Tenant↔shop setup sequence remains Cần chốt. The parties sign externally; Moderator attaches the signed agreement and assigns one physical machine, contract terms, location, deposit and installation fee. For a conventional unit, Technician records delivery and installation; only contract location and handover/service history apply. For Smart IoT, the unit already has its factory Global Menu; Technician records link/activation and the device-reported state, and Tenant sees monitoring/menus supported by that device. Moderator issues the first bill with deposit shown separately; Tenant transfers externally, uploads proof and confirms; Moderator compares to company bank records and records reconciliation. Each month Smart usage is charged only for successful cups assigned to that contract. Edge-stored late data in an ordinary month is carried to the following bill. Tenant submission timestamp stops late-charge accrual and bill reminders, while Moderator reconciliation is separately audited. Support, repair and same-type replacement are linked to the same contract and physical machines. One month before expiry the parties may discuss service; if they proceed, they close the old contract and create a new independent contract. Otherwise Moderator requests return; Technician collects and assesses; only then Moderator settles bills/damage and deposit and records an eligible external refund.

### One used-machine buyback lifecycle

Only a Customer already renting from Dolores enters the buyback process. The offered machine and eligibility are recorded as a buyback case. Technician evaluates it using internal company criteria; only then Moderator agrees the purchase, gets the external purchase agreement signed and uploads the document and external transfer evidence. Customer can view and acknowledge the evidence separately. When the agreement permits, collection may go ahead while acknowledgement remains pending, visibly marked “Chờ khách xác nhận trước khi thu gom”. Technician records collection and processing; the machine stays unavailable through refurbishment and test. After satisfactory Technician result, Moderator explicitly releases the machine into available inventory. Payment or pickup alone cannot make it rentable.
