Dolores Software Requirements Specification
Smart IoT monitoring and machine rental operations
Version 1.12 October 2026English edition
This specification translates the supplied Dolores business-flow document into software requirements covering Smart IoT monitoring and Pay As You Go, rental operations, machine history and used-machine buyback. It incorporates the user clarifications supplied on 2 October 2026 and preserves the distinction between platform functions and activities performed outside Dolores.
Document status: All 20 source main flows and 45 scenarios are represented. Version 1.1 incorporates six subsequent business decisions. Remaining decisions and review gaps are not implementation-ready merely because the source scenario list is covered.
Source authority: The supplied main-flow DOCX and the explicit clarifications identified in Section 1 control this SRS. Screen mockups, earlier estimates and unrelated project reports are not used to add requirements.
Revision scope: Preserved Tenant recipes on Global Menu updates; complete-data gating for the final Smart IoT bill; Tenant submission time as the late-charge/reminder cutoff; actual-time rental for a same-month start and end; same-type replacements; reconciled-receipt revenue and external refund records. Changes are identified by U-06 through U-11 beside affected requirements.
Use: Review, implementation planning and preparation of acceptance criteria for the confirmed scope. The acceptance observations are derived expectations, not claims that software has been implemented or tested.

1 Introduction
1 1 Purpose and scope
Dolores primarily supports remote observation of Smart IoT coffee machines and monthly PAYG charging from successful sold-cup reports. It also stores and tracks customer contracts, external payment evidence, machine inventory and lifecycle history for Smart IoT and Conventional machines. The SRS describes what the platform and connected Smart Coffee Machine must support, while leaving internal company judgments and external transactions with the responsible people.
The intended readers are the Dolores project stakeholders and the people reviewing, implementing or validating the stated requirements. This document does not choose an unapproved technology stack, payment integration or technical service level.

1 3 Requirement conventions
FR identifies a functional requirement; BR identifies a business rule; DR identifies a logical data record; IF identifies an external interface; QC identifies a source-supported quality constraint; OI identifies an open issue. MF-xx/Sxx references preserve the source flow and scenario identifiers. The word shall denotes a requirement within the confirmed scope. To be confirmed denotes missing detail and must not be implemented as an assumed business rule.
A System action belongs to Dolores unless explicitly assigned to the Smart Coffee Machine or Edge. Admin, Moderator, Technician and Tenant are human roles; System and Smart Coffee Machine are participating technical actors. Customer, Tenant and Store Owner refer to the customer-side actor in this specification.
2 Overall description
2 1 Product boundary
The platform links customers and shops to rental contracts and physical machines. A connected Smart Coffee Machine supplies the device data used for monitoring, location display, menu application results and usage billing. External bank transfers and externally signed contracts provide evidence that staff upload and reconcile. Technical assessment and scheduling remain company activities outside the platform.

2 4 Dependencies and operating context
Smart IoT uses the described Android board/tablet Edge and GPS capability. The customer must meet the company network prerequisites before a Smart rental contract proceeds. Cloud visibility and menu update results depend on the device reporting data; offline storage and later synchronization are part of the confirmed machine behavior. Device models, protocols, data capacities and reporting intervals are not specified.
Bank-account checking is internal company work performed by the Moderator. The platform receives records and evidence of external payments and refunds, with separate confirmation/reconciliation tracking; an automated bank feed or online payment gateway is not specified. The parties likewise sign contracts outside the platform.
3 Business rules
These rules apply across the functional flows. Monetary amounts are in Vietnamese dong. Contract-specific responsibility and charges are recorded from the agreed contract; this SRS does not create additional commercial penalties.

Source: MF-04, MF-05
BR-06  Smart IoT rental. The baseline Smart IoT charge is VND 750,000 per month plus VND 1,000 per valid cup.
Source: MF-04, MF-05, MF-06
BR-07  Deposit. The deposit is VND 10,000,000 per contract per machine. It is shown and tracked separately; it is not carried into a later month as part of the delayed-usage rule.
Source: MF-04, MF-11; U-03
BR-08  Installation charge. The default is VND 1,000,000 per machine, or a different installation fee agreed in the contract.

Source: MF-05

Source: MF-04, MF-05

Source: MF-05
BR-14  Overdue balance. Each day of lateness adds five percent of the previous unpaid balance, including accumulated late charges, until the Tenant has uploaded transaction evidence and clicked Confirm payment. The charge applies to the remaining unpaid amount. Reconciliation later does not move the confirmed submission cutoff. [U-08]
Source: MF-05
BR-15  Bill reminders. Send a daily reminder until the Tenant has uploaded transaction evidence and clicked Confirm payment. Stop at that completed-submission timestamp; Moderator reconciliation is separate. Exception handling for rejected/insufficient submissions remains OI-07. [U-08]
Source: MF-05
BR-16  Valid cup. A valid cup is a sold cup that the Smart Coffee Machine reports successful. A Conventional machine has no telemetry cup count or PAYG.
Source: MF-06

Source: MF-03, MF-06

The final Smart IoT bill instead waits for complete data through the rental end/use cutoff and includes all remaining unbilled cups. BR-19  Factory default menu. Only Smart IoT machines have the Dolores Global Menu, already present from manufacture. Conventional machines do not receive it.

Source: MF-07

Source: MF-07
BR-23  Preventive maintenance boundary. The company performs maintenance every three months within a ten-day window. Dolores records completed maintenance/history and does not manage the maintenance schedule or generate its reminders.

3 1 Confirmed charge calculations
For a full Conventional rental month, the base rental is VND 1,500,000. For a full Smart IoT rental month, the base is VND 750,000 and the usage charge is VND 1,000 multiplied by the valid cups assigned to the bill. Applicable service charges are added according to the contract. The first bill additionally contains the separately tracked deposit and agreed installation charge.
For a day of lateness with no intervening payment, the new unpaid balance equals the preceding unpaid balance multiplied by 1.05. The confirmed example is VND 5,000,000, then VND 5,250,000 after day one, then VND 5,512,500 after day two. Late-charge accrual stops at the completed evidence-upload and Confirm payment timestamp under U-08, separately from bank reconciliation. Rounding, partial-payment allocation and invalid/insufficient-submission exception handling remain OI-07.
Revenue is calculated from reconciled money received. Gross Revenue includes separately identified deposits; Actual Revenue excludes deposits and includes rental and applicable actual charges. Issued bills and unreviewed Tenant evidence are not recognized receipts. Refunds occur outside Dolores with linked evidence and confirmation records. Whether reports subtract refunds or display a separate refund total, and the classification of individual charge categories, remain OI-17.

4 1 Customer onboarding and rental contracts
Source flow: MF-01. Actors: Tenant, Moderator, Admin; Technician for the external prerequisite assessment.
Preconditions: A customer has expressed a rental need. For Smart IoT, the company or Technician assesses prerequisites and the customer must satisfy the network conditions before the Moderator proceeds with the contract.
Functional requirements
FR-01-01  The system shall allow the Moderator to record the agreed rental terms, including customer/shop, machine type, rental period, installation location, deposit, installation charge and applicable contractual fees.  [MF-01/S01]
FR-01-02  The system shall allow the Moderator to consult machine inventory when selecting the proposed rental machine.  [MF-01/S01]
FR-01-03  The system shall allow a Moderator to create a customer account. Admin account administration shall cover Tenant, Moderator and Technician accounts.  [MF-01/S02]
FR-01-04  The system shall allow the Moderator to create a contract record and upload the contract signed outside Dolores.  [MF-01/S02]
FR-01-05  The system shall associate a rental contract with its customer/shop and one primary physical machine. A customer may hold multiple contracts; a physical machine shall not be assigned as a primary rental machine to more than one active contract.  [MF-01/S02]
FR-01-06  The system shall allow the Tenant to view their own contract records and the Moderator to view and manage customer contract records.  [MF-01/S02]
MF-01 S01 Receive the rental need and agree the terms
1. The Tenant explains the required machine type and proposed installation location to the Moderator.
2. For Smart IoT, the company/Technician assesses prerequisites outside Dolores; the Tenant provides the required network infrastructure.
3. The Moderator discusses the machine type, rental period, location, baseline price, VND 10,000,000 deposit and installation fee, and checks inventory.
4. The parties agree the commercial terms for the externally signed contract. Dolores stores the agreed result; it does not conduct the negotiation or technical assessment.
MF-01 S02 Create the account and register the signed contract
1. Admin or Moderator creates the customer account under the confirmed account permissions. Admin manages the relevant account access and shop records.
2. The parties sign the rental contract outside Dolores.
3. The Moderator creates the contract record, links the customer/shop, records the rental terms and installation location, and uploads the signed document.
4. The Moderator assigns the primary machine, subject to the active-contract restriction. Delivery then follows MF-02 or MF-03.
Postcondition: The customer and signed contract are recorded, with the primary machine and installation location identified.
Acceptance observation: A Moderator can create a customer account without being granted administration of Moderator or Technician accounts. A second active primary assignment for the same physical machine is not accepted.
To be confirmed: OI-01, OI-02.
4 2 Conventional machine delivery and installation
Source flow: MF-02. Actors: Moderator, Technician, Tenant.
Preconditions: A Conventional machine and a registered contract with an installation location have been selected.
Functional requirements
FR-02-01  The system shall allow the Moderator to create a delivery work request containing the customer, contract, machine, installation location and handover accessories.  [MF-02/S01]
FR-02-02  The system shall allow the assigned Technician to access the delivery request.  [MF-02/S01]
FR-02-03  The system shall record the machine identifier, handover time and location, installation result and handover information entered by the Technician.  [MF-02/S02]
FR-02-04  The system shall retain the delivery result in machine/contract history and allow the Moderator to record work completion.  [MF-02/S02]
FR-02-05  The system shall not expose Smart IoT telemetry, cup counting, connection status, monitored GPS location or remote menu operations for Conventional machines. Conventional machines shall not receive the Dolores Global Menu.  [MF-02/S02]
MF-02 S01 Request delivery and assign a Technician
1. The Moderator checks the recorded contract, selected Conventional machine and installation location.
2. The Moderator creates and assigns the delivery request with the relevant handover information.
3. The Technician receives the request, prepares the machine from inventory and arranges delivery with the Tenant.
MF-02 S02 Install and record handover
1. The Technician delivers and installs the machine at the contractual installation location.
2. The Technician records the machine, time, location and installation/handover result.
3. The Tenant receives the machine; the Technician reports completion to the Moderator.
4. Dolores retains the contract, declared location and subsequent service history without creating IoT or menu data for the machine.
Postcondition: A Conventional handover is recorded and available for subsequent contract and machine-history tracking.
Acceptance observation: The Conventional machine detail shows the recorded installation location and history without an online/offline status, cup counter or Dolores menu.
4 3 Smart IoT delivery and activation
Source flow: MF-03. Actors: Moderator, Technician, Tenant, Smart Coffee Machine, System.
Preconditions: The Smart IoT prerequisites have been satisfied and the machine has a registered rental contract. Its default Global Menu is already present from manufacture.
Functional requirements
FR-03-01  The system shall allow a Smart IoT delivery request to identify the physical machine, its associated device and rental contract.  [MF-03/S01]
FR-03-02  The system shall record the device/machine/contract association and the Technician installation and activation result.  [MF-03/S01]
FR-03-03  The system shall display the available telemetry, valid-cup usage, connection status and monitored GPS location reported by the Smart Coffee Machine.  [MF-03/S01]
FR-03-04  The system shall retain the distinction between the contractual installation location and the monitored device location.  [MF-03/S02]
FR-03-05  The handover record shall capture the Technician confirmation that the machine default Dolores Global Menu is present and applied. An initial cloud download shall not be required merely to establish the existence of the factory-provided menu.  [MF-03/S02]
FR-03-06  The system shall allow the Tenant to view the menu reported as applied by the machine and shall restrict their changes to private recipes and supported private drinks.  [MF-03/S02]
FR-03-07  The system shall retain the maximum offline duration agreed in the contract; five days is a reference value, not a fixed system-wide threshold.  [MF-03/S02]
MF-03 S01 Deliver and activate the Smart IoT machine
1. The Moderator creates the Smart IoT delivery request linked to the contract and device.
2. The Technician installs the machine at the contractual location and records the machine/device association. The stated Edge hardware is an Android board and tablet with GPS.
3. The machine sends available usage, telemetry, connection and location information to Dolores.
4. The Technician reports the activation result; the Tenant and Moderator view the machine within their permitted scope.
MF-03 S02 Confirm the machine and default menu are ready
1. The Moderator checks the machine, contract and installation-location association.
2. The Technician verifies that the Dolores Global Menu already installed from manufacture is present and applied before completing handover.
3. Dolores displays the device-confirmed menu and usage associated with the contract.
4. Subsequent Global Menu updates are managed by Admin/Moderator under MF-07. Tenant customization is private and subject to actual machine support.
Postcondition: The installed Smart IoT machine is linked to the correct contract, with its default menu and activation result recorded.
Acceptance observation: A factory-provided default menu is not treated as missing simply because no first-time cloud menu push occurred. Conventional machines do not enter this flow.
To be confirmed: OI-03, OI-04.
4 4 First month billing and payment
Source flow: MF-04. Actors: Moderator, Tenant, System, Smart Coffee Machine.
Preconditions: The rental contract and applicable charges are recorded. Smart IoT usage is available to the extent it has synchronized.
Functional requirements
FR-04-01  The system shall allow the Moderator to prepare and review first-month charges for the contract.  [MF-04/S01]
FR-04-02  The first-month bill shall distinguish the VND 10,000,000 deposit from rental, installation, Smart IoT usage and other applicable contractual charges.  [MF-04/S01]
FR-04-03  The system shall use a default installation charge of VND 1,000,000 per machine or the installation amount agreed in the contract.  [MF-04/S01]
FR-04-04  The system shall apply the confirmed machine-type rental and PAYG prices. First-month rental is based on actual rental time for a mid-month start; a rental that starts and ends in the same month also uses actual rental time. [U-09]  [MF-04/S01]
FR-04-05  The Moderator shall be able to issue the first-month bill at month end and present the charge breakdown. If it is also the final Smart IoT bill, the complete-data gate in MF-05 applies. [U-07]  [MF-04/S01]
FR-04-06  The system shall allow the Tenant to submit external bank-transfer evidence against the bill and allow the Moderator to record reconciliation with the company bank account.  [MF-04/S02]
MF-04 S01 Prepare the first-month bill
1. The Moderator checks the contract and prepares the deposit, rental, installation and applicable extra charges.
2. For Smart IoT, the System supplies synchronized valid-cup usage and the PAYG calculation.
3. The first-month rental reflects actual rental time if the contract starts during the month; the precise proration formula remains OI-05.
4. The Moderator issues the itemized bill at month end, subject to the final Smart IoT complete-data gate if this is also the last bill, and sends the payment request. The deposit remains a separate line and tracked amount.
MF-04 S02 Submit evidence and reconcile payment
1. The Tenant pays by bank transfer outside Dolores and uploads payment evidence for the bill.
2. The Moderator compares the evidence with the actual company bank transaction.
3. If the amounts and transaction match, the Moderator records the reconciliation result. Otherwise, the payment remains subject to checking.
4. Shortfalls, excess payments and overdue balances follow MF-05. Dolores does not process the bank transfer.
Postcondition: The first-month bill and its payment evidence/reconciliation are recorded, with the deposit distinguishable from rental and other charges.
Acceptance observation: The VND 10,000,000 deposit remains separately identifiable in the first-month bill and is not merged into PAYG usage.
To be confirmed: OI-05, OI-06, OI-07.
4 5 Monthly billing and payment reconciliation
Source flow: MF-05. Actors: Moderator, Tenant, System, Smart Coffee Machine.
Preconditions: A contract has a monthly billing period, applicable rental terms, available usage and any contractual service charges.
Functional requirements
FR-05-01  The system shall allow ordinary monthly bills to be issued on the last day of the month. The final Smart IoT bill shall not be issued until all usage data through the rental end/use cutoff has synchronized; issuance may therefore be later than month end. Conventional bills have no online-data gate. [U-07]  [MF-05/S01]
FR-05-02  The system shall determine the payment term as ten counted days from bill issuance, counting Monday through Saturday and excluding Sunday. The exact inclusion of the issue date and holiday handling remain to be confirmed.  [MF-05/S01]
FR-05-03  A Conventional monthly bill shall include VND 1,500,000 rental and applicable contractual charges; a Smart IoT bill shall include VND 750,000 base rental, VND 1,000 per valid cup and applicable contractual charges.  [MF-05/S01]
FR-05-04  The system shall calculate rental from actual rental time when the rental starts and ends in the same month. Otherwise the existing actual-time first-month and full ending-month rules apply. Smart IoT usage shall stop at the actual usage cutoff. [U-09]  [MF-05/S01]
FR-05-05  Ordinary bills shall distinguish current-period usage from delayed prior-period valid cups carried forward. The final Smart IoT bill shall include all remaining unbilled valid usage through the cutoff after complete synchronization, without charging a cup twice. No new rental month is created to collect final delayed usage, and no deposit is carried forward by this rule. [U-07]  [MF-05/S01]
FR-05-06  The Moderator shall be able to include repair, part and labour charges that are applicable under the signed contract.  [MF-05/S01]
FR-05-07  The system shall associate uploaded transaction evidence and the Tenant Confirm payment action with the bill. It shall record the time both submission conditions are satisfied separately from the Moderator reconciliation time and result. [U-08]  [MF-05/S02]
FR-05-08  For a reconciled shortfall within the payment term, the system shall support the Moderator request for payment of the remaining amount.  [MF-05/S02]
FR-05-09  For a reconciled excess payment, the system shall track the external refund after Tenant payment confirmation and bank reconciliation, regardless of lateness. It shall retain the refund transaction, evidence and confirmation linked to the bill; Dolores does not transfer money. [U-11]  [MF-05/S02]
FR-05-10  For an overdue unpaid balance, the system shall calculate the confirmed daily cumulative five-percent charge up to the time the Tenant has uploaded transaction evidence and clicked Confirm payment. Later Moderator reconciliation shall not replace that submission cutoff. Shortfall/rejected-evidence exceptions remain OI-07. [U-08]  [MF-05/S02]
FR-05-11  The System shall stop daily bill reminders when the Tenant has uploaded transaction evidence and clicked Confirm payment, while retaining a separate pending-reconciliation result. The first reminder date remains OI-08; invalid/shortfall resumption rules remain OI-07. [U-08]  [MF-05/S02]
FR-05-12  The System shall prevent final Smart IoT bill issuance until usage through the rental end/use cutoff is confirmed complete, including applicable primary/replacement intervals and offline days. It shall derive the payment deadline from the actual issue date. The completeness evidence is specified further under OI-09. [U-07]  [MF-05/S01]
MF-05 S01 Close the month and issue the bill
1. The System makes available synchronized usage and the Moderator checks contract rental and applicable service charges.
2. The bill separates rental, usage, prior-period late usage and other applicable charges; deposits are tracked separately.
3. At month end, the Moderator issues the ordinary bill and payment request. For the final Smart IoT bill, the Moderator waits for complete usage data through the cutoff before issuance. The ten-counted-day payment term starts from the actual issue date.
4. For ordinary monthly billing, delayed cups are included in the following month. Before final Smart IoT issuance, pending offline usage must synchronize and previously billed cups must be excluded. Waiting for synchronization does not extend the rental period or the usage cutoff.
MF-05 S02 Reconcile payment and handle differences
1. The Tenant transfers money externally, uploads transaction evidence and clicks Confirm payment. The System records the completed-submission time and stops further late-charge accrual and bill reminders at that time; bank reconciliation is still pending.
2. The Moderator checks the company bank account internally and records the reconciliation.
3. If payment is insufficient within the term, the Moderator requests the balance. If payment exceeds the amount due, the Moderator returns the excess after confirmation and reconciliation.
4. Before the completed Tenant submission cutoff, an overdue unpaid amount accumulates five percent daily on the preceding balance. With no intervening payment or completed submission, VND 5,000,000 becomes VND 5,250,000 and then VND 5,512,500.
5. The System keeps the submission timestamp separate from Moderator reconciliation. Pending reconciliation alone does not continue reminders or late-charge accrual after a completed Tenant submission. If the submission is invalid or insufficient, the outstanding amount remains; restart/backdating behavior requires OI-07.
Postcondition: The issued bill, outstanding balance, evidence, reconciliation and any excess refund remain traceable to the contract.
Acceptance observation: Ordinary late usage is carried forward. If a rental ends on day 30 and its last two days synchronize on day 2 of the next month, its final Smart IoT bill is withheld until the data is complete and includes the unbilled usage. The waiting period adds no rental days. The payment term starts from actual issuance. A VND 5,000,000 overdue balance follows the confirmed two-day example until the Tenant submission cutoff.
To be confirmed: OI-05, OI-06, OI-07, OI-08, OI-09.

1. Admin/Moderator manages the Dolores source menu, its drinks, mandatory ingredients and base recipes.
2. Changes are limited to recipes and capabilities supported by the relevant Smart IoT machine.
3. The System stores the internal menu configuration. Tenant access does not include creating, editing or issuing the Global Menu.
MF-07 S02 Apply an internal menu update
1. Admin/Moderator selects the Smart IoT target and sends a supported Global Menu update. The initial default menu already exists from manufacture.
2. The machine receives the update, attempts application and reports the result.
3. While awaiting a successful response, Dolores continues to show the last device-confirmed menu and the pending or failed result separately.
4. On success, the applied menu becomes visible to the Tenant. On failure, the Tenant may notify the Moderator, who coordinates a Technician.
5. The System preserves the Tenant private recipes and drinks and updates the separately displayed base recipe. For example, a private 60 ml Espresso remains 60 ml when the base changes from 40 ml to 50 ml. The Tenant may choose to adopt the 50 ml base; any device application still requires machine success confirmation. Incompatible or removed base items remain OI-12.
MF-07 S03 Customize a private recipe
1. The Tenant selects their own Smart IoT machine and a drink from its Dolores Global Menu.
2. The Tenant changes supported ingredient quantities while retaining mandatory ingredients; a fresh-milk coffee must retain both coffee and fresh milk.
3. The System presents the base and proposed recipe and checks machine support.
4. The Tenant confirms saving and sending the private recipe to the selected machine.
5. The System reports the machine result and marks the private recipe as applied only after success. No mandatory Moderator review is added.
MF-07 S04 Add a supported private drink
1. The Tenant selects a Smart IoT machine already carrying the Dolores menu and enters the private drink, ingredients and recipe.
2. The System checks the machine capabilities and supported quantities.
3. If unsupported or not verifiable, the System explains the unmet condition and prevents application.
4. If supported, the Tenant confirms saving and sending the private drink.
5. The machine reports success or failure. The drink remains private and does not become a Global Menu item.
Postcondition: Internal Global Menu ownership is preserved. Existing Tenant private recipes and drinks survive base-menu updates; the Tenant can view and choose the new base recipe. Device-confirmed application remains distinct from cloud updates.
Acceptance observation: A Tenant cannot modify the Global Menu. A Global change from 40 ml to 50 ml preserves a Tenant private 60 ml recipe. The updated base is visible and can be explicitly selected; unsupported changes cannot be applied, and a successful send alone is not successful application.
To be confirmed: OI-03, OI-12.

6 2 Relationships and integrity
A customer can hold multiple contracts. Each primary assignment links one physical machine to one active rental contract. A temporary same-type replacement is an additional temporary link to that contract, not a new rental contract.
Contractual location belongs to the contract/installation record. Smart monitored location belongs to the device report. They are stored and displayed as different facts.
Usage attribution must follow the actual machine-use interval. Ordinary late receipt changes the billing period under carry-forward without changing the originating customer/contract. Final Smart IoT billing waits for complete usage through the cutoff and includes remaining unbilled cups; synchronization delay does not extend the rental.
A private recipe/drink retains its Tenant/machine scope and its distinction from the Dolores Global Menu. It cannot modify another Tenant data or the internal source menu. Global updates preserve the private values while making the new base available for optional Tenant adoption.
Bills, payment evidence, reconciliation and refund tracking remain linked. A deposit is distinguishable from rental/PAYG and is settled independently for each contract.
Machine service history follows the physical machine across handovers and contracts. Bought-back machines retain their purchase association through refurbishment, testing and release to inventory.

8 Limitations and exclusions
EX-01 External contract signing
Dolores stores rental and purchase agreements signed outside the platform. Electronic contract signing is excluded.
EX-02 External payment and refunds
Customer rent payments, purchase payments and refunds are carried out externally. Dolores stores evidence, reconciliation and recorded outcomes; online payment processing and automatic bank transfers are not in scope.
EX-03 Conventional machine capabilities
Conventional machines have contractual records and service history but no telemetry, cup counts, digital twin, online/offline status, live GPS, remote recipes or Dolores Global Menu.
EX-04 Maintenance scheduling
The company maintenance cycle is every three months within ten days. Planning and scheduling occur outside Dolores; the platform records completed maintenance and does not generate maintenance reminders.
EX-05 Internal assessment
Prerequisite assessment, diagnosis, damage assessment, valuation and physical test criteria are company/Technician procedures. Dolores records their outcomes; it does not configure scoring or independently determine a physical test result.
EX-06 Contractual enforcement
Liability, repair/part charges, early-termination consequences, violation penalties and non-cooperation handling follow the external contract/company process. No universal forfeiture rate, automatic machine lock or enforcement workflow is introduced.
EX-07 Renewal
Continued rental uses a newly signed independent contract. Renewal or extension of the previous contract is excluded.
EX-08 Buyback eligibility and readiness
Buyback support is restricted to Dolores rental customers under company criteria. Collection or payment does not by itself make a machine available for rent.
EX-09 Manufacturing and unlisted capabilities
The Smart Global Menu is factory-provided. This describes the machine starting condition and does not add manufacturing management. Procurement, coffee supply, ingredient-stock measurement or physical machine redesign are not specified as platform features in the approved source.
EX-10 Unspecified implementation details
This SRS does not select a technology stack, communication protocol, payment provider, cloud topology, 3D Smart visualization feature or numeric service level absent from the approved source. Such decisions require separate confirmation.

Appendix A Terminology
Term
Meaning in this specification
Admin
System Administrator; manages Tenant, Moderator and Technician accounts and performs the confirmed internal menu/overview functions.
Tenant or Customer
The Dolores rental customer; also called Store Owner in the source. Own-record access applies.
Conventional
A rental coffee machine without the described Smart IoT telemetry, cup billing, monitored GPS or Dolores menu capability.
Smart IoT
A connected coffee machine with the described Edge, device data, GPS, menu and PAYG capabilities, subject to actual hardware support.
Edge
The described machine-side Android board/tablet environment that provides local data retention and synchronization.
PAYG
Pay As You Go: the VND 1,000 charge per valid sold cup, in addition to Smart monthly base rental.
Valid cup
A sold cup reported successful by the Smart Coffee Machine.
Global Menu
The factory-provided Smart IoT source menu managed by Admin/Moderator. Updates preserve Tenant private values; the new base can be viewed and selected.
Private recipe or drink
A Tenant-specific supported customization or new drink for their own Smart IoT machine; it does not alter Global Menu ownership.
Contract location
The installation location declared in the contract, available for both machine types.
Monitored location
The separate GPS location reported by a Smart IoT device.
Replacement
A same-type temporary machine associated with the original rental contract during maintenance/repair.
Reconciliation
Moderator comparison of submitted transfer evidence with the company bank transaction and recording of the result.
Deposit
VND 10,000,000 per contract/machine, shown separately and settled only after the required recovery/assessment sequence.
Gross Revenue
The user-defined total based on reconciled money received, including deposits; refund presentation remains OI-17.
Actual Revenue
Reconciled rental receipts and applicable actual charges excluding deposits; classification/refund presentation remain OI-17.
Buyback
Company purchase of a used machine from an eligible Dolores rental customer, followed by collection, refurbishment and testing.
Available for rent
The recorded condition permitting rental allocation; for bought-back machines, it requires satisfactory Technician refurbishment/testing and Moderator release.
To be confirmed
A detail not settled by the source/clarifications. It must not be treated as an approved implementation rule.
